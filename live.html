How to join

Step 1. Wait until the room appears in the box above.

Step 2. If the room shows a sign-in or name screen, type your name as you want others to see it. You do not need an account.

Step 3. Click Join inside the room. Only then will you enter the session.

Step 4. Allow the microphone and camera when your browser asks, only if you want to speak or be seen. You can turn them off at any time.

Before you join

Be respectful to everyone in the room. Speak one at a time, keep to the topic of the session, and let the host manage the order of questions.

Do not share other members’ personal information, and do not share recordings or screenshots that identify people without their agreement. Members who disrupt a session may be asked to leave, and reports can be made through the forum.

Topic rooms

Topic-specific rooms, such as International Relations or Peace and Security, can be found on the Categories page. Use the General Room for sessions that cover more than one area.
